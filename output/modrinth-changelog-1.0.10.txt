Compatibility fix for Peterwolfs Splinecart rails:

- Locomotives now apply power directly along the spline instead of fighting the carrier entity.
- Linked carts now share and constrain their real along-track speed on curved spline sections.
- Braking, spacing correction, speed limits, and smoke detection now use the spline follower's movement.
- Position corrections no longer displace minecarts independently from their spline follower.
